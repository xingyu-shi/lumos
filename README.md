# lumos 🌟
Unlock the power of your notes and conversations

Lumos is an intelligent tool designed to analyze your chat history, personal notes, and documents. It helps you transform scattered information into structured insights. With Lumos, you can:
	•	Build Knowledge Graphs: Automatically extract entities and relationships to create an evolving web of knowledge.
	•	Perform Advanced Text Analysis: Identify key topics, keywords, and sentiment from your texts.
	•	Search Smarter: Utilize semantic and contextual search to find the exact information you need.
	•	Summarize Effortlessly: Generate concise summaries from long conversations and notes.

Lumos empowers you to discover, organize, and understand your information like never before. 🚀
